Key points

  • The tanks sit below the engine, so a pump has to be working for fuel to reach it. The engine-driven pump is the main one, and the electric pump backs it up.
  • The selector feeds the engine from one tank at a time: L, R, or OFF. There's no BOTH. To select OFF, you press the button first.
  • The fuel pump goes on for engine start, takeoff, landing, and switching tanks. It's off in cruise, so a failed engine-driven pump shows up right away.
  • The mixture sets the ratio of fuel to air. Peak EGT is best economy, 100°F rich of peak is best power, and idle cutoff stops the fuel.
  • Each tank holds 24 gallons usable, or 17 when the fuel is at the bottom of the tab in the filler neck.
