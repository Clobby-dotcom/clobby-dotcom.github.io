Flows

A flow is the pattern you run from memory at each phase of a flight. The checklist then confirms you didn't miss anything.

Before start

POH: Before starting engine

Owner's Manual: Before starting the engine

POH 4.5c: Before starting engine

  1. Preflight and passenger briefing done.
  2. Seats and belts adjusted and locked. Brakes tested and set.
  3. Circuit breakers in, including the avionics breakers.
  4. Electrical equipment off. Avionics master off, so the start can't damage the radios.
  5. Fuel selector on BOTH. Fuel shutoff valve pushed full in.
  1. Seats and belts adjusted and locked.
  2. Fuel selector on BOTH.
  3. Brakes tested and set.
  4. Radios and electrical equipment off.
  1. Flaps up. Door closed and secure. Seats locked, belts fastened.
  2. Fuel selector on the tank you want. Parking brake set.
  3. Circuit breakers in. Alternate air closed. Alternate static source off.
  4. Every electrical switch off, then the battery master off.

Engine start

POH: Starting engine (with battery)

Owner's Manual: Starting the engine

POH 4.5d: Engine start, cold engine

  1. Throttle open a quarter inch. Mixture at idle cutoff.
  2. Prop area clear. Master on, beacon on.
  3. Cold engine only, prime: auxiliary fuel pump on, mixture full rich until the fuel flow is steady (3 to 5 seconds), back to idle cutoff, pump off.
  4. Crank. As it starts, bring the mixture smoothly to rich.
  5. Oil pressure up. Then nav lights as needed, avionics master and radios on, flaps up.

Flooded: pump off, mixture at idle cutoff, throttle half to full open, and crank. When it starts, mixture rich and throttle back.

  1. Mixture rich. Carburetor heat cold.
  2. Prime 2 to 6 strokes, or none if the engine is warm. Lock the primer.
  3. Throttle open an eighth of an inch.
  4. Master on. Prop area clear.
  5. Ignition to START, and let go when it fires. Oil pressure up.
  1. Throttle half an inch open.
  2. Battery master, alternator, both magnetos, fuel pump, and strobes on.
  3. Prime: mixture forward for 3 to 5 seconds, then back to idle cutoff.
  4. Look over the PFD annunciations. Clear the prop, then press ENGINE START.
  5. As it fires, mixture forward and set the throttle. Oil pressure up.
  6. Warm up at 800 to 1,200 RPM.

Hot engine: the same flow without the prime. Flooded: throttle full open and fuel pump off.

Before taxi and taxi

POH

Owner's Manual

POH 4.5e and 4.5f: Before taxiing, Taxiing

The POH has no separate before-taxi checklist. Its engine start checklist ends with lights, avionics, and flaps up.

The manual has no separate before-taxi checklist.

  1. Databases current. Look over the CAS messages, PFD annunciations, and system messages.
  2. Enter the fuel on the Fuel Calc tab and the weights on the weight and balance page.
  3. Lights, vents, radios, and avionics set. Autopilot self-test passed, if it's installed.
  4. Standby instrument on with no red X's. Altimeters set. Passengers briefed.
  5. Parking brake off. Taxi area clear, power in slowly, then check the brakes and steering.

Runup

POH

Owner's Manual

POH 4.5g: Ground check

The POH has no separate runup checklist. The runup is part of Before takeoff, below.

The manual has no separate runup checklist. The runup is part of Before takeoff, below.

  1. Parking brake set. Throttle to 2,000 RPM.
  2. Each magneto: a drop of 175 RPM at most.
  3. Oil temperature and pressure checked. Volts 28, give or take 1. Alternator amps normal.
  4. Alternate air: look for an RPM drop.
  5. Fuel pump off. Throttle back.
  6. With the autopilot: engage it, make sure you can overpower it, and check that the disconnect button works.

Before takeoff

POH: Before takeoff

Owner's Manual: Before take-off

POH 4.5h: Before takeoff

  1. Parking brake set. Seat backs upright, seats and belts secure, doors closed and locked.
  2. Flight controls free and correct. Flight instruments checked and set.
  3. Fuel quantity checked. Mixture rich. Fuel selector on BOTH.
  4. Runup at 1,800 RPM: each magneto (150 RPM drop at most, 50 between them), vacuum, engine instruments, and ammeter.
  5. No annunciators lit. Throttle to idle to check it, then 1,000 RPM or less.
  6. Friction lock, strobes, radios, and avionics set. Autopilot off, if it's installed.
  7. Trim set for takeoff. Flaps 0° to 10°. Brakes off.
  1. Parking brake set. Flight controls free and correct.
  2. Fuel selector on BOTH. Trim at the takeoff setting.
  3. Runup at 1,700 RPM: engine instruments and ammeter, suction at 4.6 to 5.4 inches of mercury, each magneto (125 RPM drop at most, 50 between them), and carburetor heat.
  4. Flight instruments and radios set. Autopilot or wing leveler off, if one is installed.
  5. Doors and window closed and locked.
  1. Battery master, alternator, and both magnetos on. Fuel pump on.
  2. Flight instruments and the standby checked. Messages and annunciations looked over. Engine indications checked.
  3. Fuel selector on the proper tank. Alternate air closed. Mixture set.
  4. Seat backs up, seats locked, belts on. Fasten the belts on any empty seat.
  5. Flaps set. Stabilator and rudder trim set. Controls free and correct. Door latched.

Takeoff

POH: Normal takeoff

Owner's Manual: Normal take-off

POH 4.5i: Takeoff, normal technique

  1. Flaps 0° to 10°.
  2. Full throttle. Mixture rich, or leaned for maximum RPM above 3,000 ft.
  3. Lift the nose wheel at 55 KIAS.
  4. Climb at 70 to 80 KIAS.
  1. Flaps up. Carburetor heat cold.
  2. Full throttle.
  3. Lift the nose wheel at 60 MPH.
  4. Climb at 75 to 85 MPH.
  1. Flaps up. Trim set.
  2. Hold the brakes, full power, release.
  3. Rotate smoothly at 60 KIAS.

Owner's Manual: Climb

POH 4.5j: Climb

  1. Flaps up.
  2. Climb at 70 to 85 KIAS with full throttle.
  3. Mixture rich. Above 3,000 ft, lean for maximum RPM.
  1. Climb at 80 to 90 MPH with full throttle.
  2. Mixture full rich. It may be leaned above 5,000 ft.
  1. Climb at 76 KIAS for best rate, 64 for best angle, or 87 enroute.

Cruise

POH: Cruise

Owner's Manual: Cruising

POH 4.5k: Cruise

  1. Power 2,000 to 2,400 RPM. No more than 80 percent is recommended.
  2. Trim.
  3. Lean the mixture.
  1. Power 2,200 to 2,700 RPM.
  2. Trim.
  3. Lean the mixture for maximum RPM.
  1. Set power from the power table.
  2. Lean the mixture.

Descent

POH: Descent

Owner's Manual: Let-down

POH 4.5l: Descent

  1. Power as desired. Mixture adjusted for smooth running.
  2. Altimeter set. NAV/GPS switch set.
  3. Fuel selector on BOTH.
  4. Flaps as desired: 10° below 110 KIAS, more below 85 KIAS.
  1. Mixture rich.
  2. Power as desired.
  3. Carburetor heat as needed to keep ice out.
  1. Normal descent: 2,500 RPM, 122 KIAS, mixture rich.
  2. Power-off descent: throttle closed, and verify power with the throttle every 30 seconds.

Before landing

POH: Before landing

Owner's Manual: Before landing

POH 4.5m: Approach and landing

  1. Seat backs upright. Seats and belts secure.
  2. Fuel selector on BOTH. Mixture rich.
  3. Landing and taxi lights on.
  4. Autopilot off, if it's installed.
  1. Fuel selector on BOTH. Mixture rich.
  2. Carburetor heat full on before you close the throttle.
  3. Flaps as desired.
  4. 70 to 80 MPH with flaps up, 65 to 75 MPH with flaps down.
  1. Radios and avionics set. Altimeters set.
  2. Seat backs up, belts on, armrests stowed.
  3. Fuel pump on. Fuel selector on the proper tank. Mixture full rich.
  4. Flaps as needed, below 102 KIAS. Alternate air and landing light as required.
  5. Parking brake off. Press the toe brakes to check them. Autopilot off, above 200 ft AGL.

  1. 65 to 75 KIAS with flaps up, 60 to 70 KIAS with flaps down.
  2. Touch down on the main wheels first.
  3. Lower the nose wheel gently. Brake no more than you need.
  1. Touch down on the main wheels first.
  2. Lower the nose wheel gently. Brake no more than you need.
  1. Initial approach at 75 to 95 KIAS. Final at 66 KIAS with flaps 40°.

  3. Brake as needed.

Shutdown

POH: Securing airplane

Owner's Manual: Securing aircraft

POH 4.5p: Stopping engine

  1. Parking brake set.
  2. Electrical equipment, autopilot, and avionics master off.
  3. Mixture to idle cutoff. Ignition off, then master off.
  4. Control lock in.
  5. Fuel selector on LEFT or RIGHT, so the tanks don't cross-feed.
  1. Parking brake set.
  2. Radios and electrical equipment off.
  3. Mixture to idle cutoff. Ignition and master off.
  4. Control lock in.
  1. Parking brake set. Flaps up. Fuel pump off.
  2. Electrical switches off. Alternator off.
  3. Throttle closed. Mixture to idle cutoff. Both magnetos off.
  4. Lights off, then the battery master off.

Summarized from the normal procedures in the Pilot 100i POH (VB-2960, Section 4), the Cessna 172R POH (Section 4, pages 4-11 to 4-17), and the 1970 Cessna 172 Owner's Manual (Section I).

Each step is shortened and several checklist items are often grouped into one, so this page leaves out detail the checklists have. Short-field and soft-field takeoffs and landings are on the maneuvers page.

For study only. In the airplane, the POH and current FAA publications always take priority.
